Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about MD Store

How do I place an order on MD Store?+
Browse our products, add them to your cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your delivery address and payment details, and click "Place Order". You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately.
How can I track my order?+
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can also track your order from the "My Orders" section in your account dashboard.
Can I modify or cancel my order?+
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placing them. After that, if the order has been processed for shipping, cancellation may not be possible. Contact our support team immediately for assistance.
What are the shipping charges?+
Shipping is completely FREE for orders above ₹5000. For orders below ₹5000, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies. Express delivery options are available at additional charges depending on your location.
How long does delivery take?+
Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Metro cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Chennai typically receive orders within 2-4 business days. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available for select locations.
Do you deliver to all states in India?+
Yes! We deliver to all 28 states and union territories across India. Some remote areas may have slightly longer delivery times. Enter your PIN code during checkout to check delivery availability and estimated dates.
What is the return policy?+
We offer a hassle-free 15-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can initiate a return within 15 days of delivery. Products must be in their original condition with all tags and packaging intact.
How do I initiate a return?+
Go to "My Orders" in your account, select the order, click "Return", choose the reason, and submit. Our team will arrange a pickup within 2-3 business days. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the product.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, and popular digital wallets. All online payments are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
Is it safe to enter my card details on MD Store?+
Absolutely. MD Store uses industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store your card details on our servers. All transactions are processed through certified payment gateways that comply with PCI DSS standards.
How do I create an account?+
Click "Sign In" in the header and then "Create Account". Enter your name, email, and password. Verify your email, and you're ready to shop! New users get a 10% discount using code WELCOME.
I forgot my password. What do I do?+
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and we'll send a password reset link. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact our support team.
